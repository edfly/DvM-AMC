\textbf{Conformal factor analysis on DD:} $\lambda = 2.31 \pm 0.08$, $\|x\| = 0.37 \pm 0.04$. Notably, 100\% of DD embeddings lie in the $\lambda < 2.5$ regime---confirming that DD, like ogbn-arxiv, concentrates near the Poincar\'e origin. This corroborates the adaptive property claim from the main paper.

\subsubsection*{Stage 3: Robustness}

\textbf{Gradient noise sensitivity (cosine to reference gradient):}
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lcc}
\toprule
\textbf{Noise $\sigma$} & \textbf{ogbn-arxiv cos} & \textbf{DD cos} \\
\midrule
0.00 & 1.0000 & 1.0000 \\
0.01 & 0.9827 & 0.9859 \\
0.05 & 0.7203 & 0.6829 \\
0.10 & 0.3938 & 0.2852 \\
0.20 & 0.2320 & 0.2764 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\textbf{DvM truncation stability:} Cosine similarity = 1.000 across all $M \in \{2,4,6,8\}$ on both datasets. Relative error decreases monotonically: 0.771 ($M{=}2$) $\to$ 0.570 ($M{=}8$), consistent with Theorem 1's prediction $\epsilon_{\text{grad}} \propto \rho^{T-M}$.

\subsubsection*{Stage 4: Scalability}

\textbf{Memory scaling ($M{=}8$):} Confirmed on both ogbn-arxiv and DD---stored states plateau at 9 for $T \geq 8$. At $T{=}128$, DvM achieves $14\times$ reduction (only 7\% of full BPTT memory).

\textbf{Confidence interval (5 runs on DD):} $0.5513 \pm 0.0301$ (95\% CI, t-distribution with df=4).

\subsection{Reproducibility Notes}

\begin{itemize}
    \item All experiments use Python 3.13, PyTorch 2.13 (CPU), PyTorch Geometric 2.8.0.
    \item Random seeds are controlled via \texttt{spikergnn.utils.set\_seed()} for reproducibility.
    \item Hyperparameters: $\text{hidden\_dim}=128$, $\text{num\_layers}=3$, $\tau=20$, $\alpha=0.9$ (LIF membrane decay), $c=1.0$ (Poincar\'e curvature).
    \item Episodic training: $N$-way $K$-shot $Q$-query with $Q=5$, following~\citep{Snell17}.
    \item Datasets are publicly available:
    \begin{itemize}
        \item MUTAG, PROTEINS, ENZYMES, DD: PyTorch Geometric \texttt{TUDataset}
        \item ogbn-arxiv: Open Graph Benchmark (OGB)
    \end{itemize}
\end{itemize}
